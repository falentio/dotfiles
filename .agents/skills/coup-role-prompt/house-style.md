# House Style

Every role prompt copies this scaffold verbatim, filling only the `{...}` slots. This file is the single source of truth for the set's look — edit the style here, never inside a prompt.

## Scaffold

> A full-body single-character illustration on a plain background of the {ROLE}, {BRIEF}, rendered as a crisp 2D cartoon in a modern editorial-propaganda-poster style with bold confident slightly tapered ink-style outlines of uniform stroke weight and occasional thick accent lines, flat cel shading of two to three hard-edged tonal steps per form with no soft gradients or airbrush blending, flat vector-like color fills contained inside the lines, a single warm rim light on one side, subtle paper grain and light halftone dots in the shadows only for a retro print feel, semi-stylized graphic-novel poster anatomy with broad shoulders, slightly chunky hands, an expressive simplified face and big readable eyes, and a {PALETTE} palette, carrying a {MOOD} mood, vertically centered in a 2:3 portrait frame at crisp clean high-contrast poster-print quality, avoiding {NEGATIVES}.

## Slots

- **{ROLE}** — the role name, as the character.
- **{BRIEF}** — the character: era, build, clothing, expression, pose, the tell, and the light.
- **{PALETTE}** — the role's five named colors, from the roster.
- **{MOOD}** — the role's mood phrase, from the roster.
- **{NEGATIVES}** — the list below, verbatim.

## Negatives

> photorealism, 3D render, CGI, anime moe style, chibi proportions, gore, blood, graphic violence, modern firearms or clothing, sunglasses, text, watermarks, signatures, logos, card borders, banners, frames, cluttered backgrounds, soft blurry gradients, oil-painting texture, muddy colors, extra limbs, extra fingers, distorted faces and dark horror lighting
